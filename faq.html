FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about saving with DK Travel Club, using your credit, and becoming an Ambassador.

How does the DK Travel Club savings plan work?+

You choose a contribution amount and schedule (weekly, biweekly, or monthly). Every payment builds your travel credit balance, which you apply toward a Destination Konpa package whenever you're ready to book.

What happens if I don't travel right away?+

No problem. Your credit stays valid for two years from the date it's added, so you can save on your own timeline.

Can I pause my plan?+

Yes. You can pause contributions at any time from your member dashboard and resume whenever you're ready.

Can I change my destination after I start saving?+

Your credit isn't tied to a single trip. You can apply it to any available destination or package at the time you book.

How do I become a DK Ambassador?+

Sign up through the Ambassador page. Once approved, you'll get a referral link and dashboard to track referrals and commissions.

How do I reach customer support?+

Call 321-978-2525 or email info@destinationkonpa.com. Our team typically responds within one business day.
